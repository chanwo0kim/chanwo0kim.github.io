Research Intern, AI-Robot Research Center, Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST)

I am a research intern at the AI-Robot Research Center of the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST), working with Dr. Hwasup Lim. I received my M.S. in AI-Robotics from the University of Science and Technology (UST), KIST School, and my B.S. in Mathematics and B.Eng. in Big Data from Kyungpook National University.

My research is on robot learning for dexterous manipulation. I am interested in how robots can learn new, complex tasks from a few teleoperated demonstrations, and how the 3D structure of a scene can guide the generation of additional training data. Before moving to robotics, I worked on 3D vision, including the 3D understanding of two hands manipulating objects and editable 4D scene generation with Gaussian Splatting.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Teleoperation and Data Generation for Dexterous Humanoid Manipulation
Ongoing project, KIST Robot learning

I built a whole-body teleoperation pipeline in Isaac Sim for a humanoid with dexterous hands, using a PICO VR headset and MANUS Metagloves Pro Haptic gloves. I have used it for whole-body tasks and for bimanual deformable-object manipulation such as rope knotting.

Collecting many demonstrations for every new task is not practical, so I am now working on an agentic AI approach that expands a few teleoperated demonstrations into a larger set of training data, with a focus on how the 3D structure of the scene should be taken into account during generation.

Publications

Edit4D: Editable 4D Scene Generation with Textual Control
Chanwoo Kim, Tackgeun You, Hwasup Lim
arXiv, 2026
Paper Project Page

Edit4D generates a 4D scene from a text instruction by generating a background scene and a dynamic character separately and composing them in 4D Gaussian Splatting. Because the two parts stay separate, the character or the background can be edited after generation without regenerating the whole scene.

Text-Controlled 4D Human Generation
Chanwoo Kim, Sanghun Kim, Hwasup Lim
Journal of the Korea Society of Computer and Information (KCI), 2026
Paper

A text-to-4D human generation pipeline that separates appearance and motion diffusion and combines multi-view expansion with Gaussian Splatting-based 4D reconstruction.

Splatter Layout: Geometry-embedded 3D Reconstruction via Surface Unfolding
Bryan Heryanto, Tackgeun You, Chanwoo Kim, Hwasup Lim
IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV), 2026
Paper

A single-view, feed-forward method that predicts Gaussian splats, normals, and UV maps on an unfolded surface layout. From this layout, 3D splats, point clouds, and meshes can be reconstructed and used for animation and appearance editing.

Leveraging 2D Masked Reconstruction for Domain Adaptation of 3D Pose Estimation
Hansoo Park, Chanwoo Kim, Jihyeon Kim, Hoseong Cho, Nhat Nguyen Bao Truong, Taehwan Kim, Seungryul Baek
arXiv, 2025
Paper

An unsupervised domain adaptation framework for 3D human and hand pose estimation that uses unlabeled images through masked image modeling, with foreground-centric reconstruction and attention regularization.

Class-Wise Buffer Management for Incremental Object Detection: An Effective Buffer Training Strategy
Junsu Kim, Sumin Hong, Chanwoo Kim, Jihyeon Kim, Yihalem Yimolal Tiruneh, Jeongwan On, Jihyun Song, Sunhwa Choi, Seungryul Baek
IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing (ICASSP), 2024

RMFER: Semi-supervised Contrastive Learning for Facial Expression Recognition with Reaction Mashup Video
Yunseong Cho, Chanwoo Kim, Hoseong Cho, Yunhoe Ku, Eunseo Kim, Muhammadjon Boboev, Joonseok Lee, Seungryul Baek
IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV), 2024 OralBest Paper Award Finalist
Paper Code

A semi-supervised contrastive learning framework that learns facial expression features from unlabeled reaction mashup videos, in which several people react to the same scene, by using faces from the same moment as positive pairs.

Transformer-based Unified Recognition of Two Hands Manipulating Objects
Hoseong Cho, Chanwoo Kim, Jihyeon Kim, Seongyeong Lee, Elkhan Ismayilzada, Seungryul Baek
IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), 2023
Paper

A unified Transformer framework that estimates the 3D poses of two hands and an object, the object type, and the hand-object interaction class from egocentric video. Contact maps between the hands and the object are used to recognize the interaction.

Transformer-based Global 3D Hand Pose Estimation in Two Hands Manipulating Objects Scenarios
Hoseong Cho, Donguk Kim, Chanwoo Kim, Seongyeong Lee, Seungryul Baek
Technical report, ECCV 2022 HBHA Challenge 1st Place
Report

Our first-place solution for egocentric 3D hand pose estimation: end-to-end Transformer-based estimation of the global 3D poses of two hands interacting with an object.
